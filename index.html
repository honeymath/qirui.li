Qirui Li

Assistant Professor
Department of Mathematics
Pohang University of Science and Technology
Pohang, Gyeongbuk, Korea

I work in number theory and arithmetic geometry. My interests are the relative trace formula approach to Gross–Zagier type formulas, the linear arithmetic fundamental lemma, and the geometry of Lubin–Tate and Rapoport–Zink spaces.
