\section{Research Interests}

Number theory and arithmetic geometry: automorphic representations and
relative trace formulae, the linear arithmetic fundamental lemma, elliptic
curves and $p$-divisible groups, and the geometry of Lubin--Tate,
Rapoport--Zink and Shimura varieties.
